The project particularly acknowledges:

- the PX4 and Dronecode communities for the ULog format, uORB message definitions, documentation, and technical discussion;

- the maintainers of Python, NumPy, Pandas, PyArrow, Pydantic, Typer, Rich, PyYAML, Ruff, mypy, pytest, and the wider packaging ecosystem;
