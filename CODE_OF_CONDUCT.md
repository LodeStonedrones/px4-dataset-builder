# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Standards

Positive behavior includes empathy, respect for differing viewpoints, constructive feedback, accepting responsibility, and focusing on the community. Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, sexualized language or imagery, trolling, insults, publishing private information without permission, or other conduct reasonably considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and interactions inconsistent with this Code. Report unacceptable behavior privately through the repository's maintainer contact or security reporting channel. Complaints will be reviewed promptly, fairly, and with respect for reporter privacy and security.
